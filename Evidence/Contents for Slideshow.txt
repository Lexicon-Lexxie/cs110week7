Story: Greenward: A Guide for New Garden Operators

Part 1 — Arrival
I arrived in Greenward from Kairova’s tech department to help Sana Vey and her new garden crew. The protected pods were still working, but outside, the forest had taken over the old roads and buildings. This caused the connection and systems to go dowm. The garden operators needed our guidance.
Part 2 — The Old Instructions
Sana showed me an old instruction sheet. It said to connect to the network and open the online garden system. But the network was down.
“This guide is outdated,” Sana said.
She was right. The crew needed instructions that worked even without a connection. 
Part 3 — The Way Forward
I saved a simple garden guide on their Windows computer and printed a backup copy. Now, when the network works, they can use the online system. When it doesn't, they can open the offline guide and keep working.
Sana smiled. “Now we're ready.”
And the new Greenward crew got back to growing.
